# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People drawing, sketching ideas, or writing quick visual notes on laptops and touchscreens. The original owner uses a personal single-file canvas; this project makes that workflow usable by anyone, especially with fingers and a stylus.

## Product Purpose

Provide an immediate, reliable drawing surface with all nine original tools, clear controls, smooth strokes, and local autosave. Success means users can reach every tool, draw naturally, and keep their work across reloads and device rotation.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, tactile, capable. A drawing studio that leaves room for the user's work.

## Anti-references

Avoid the original crowded, centered overflowing glass toolbar, arbitrary filled icons, controls that depend on hover or long presses, and decorative panels that shrink the drawing area.

## Design Principles

1. The canvas is the main event.
2. Every control is reachable with a finger, pointer, or keyboard.
3. Preserve existing features and saved artwork during the migration.
4. Trust comes from predictable drawing, history, and visible save feedback.
5. Artwork stays on the device, with PNG export for portability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labeled buttons, visible focus, adequate contrast, minimum 44-pixel touch targets, reduced-motion support, native dialog focus management, and screen-reader status updates. Support mouse, finger, and stylus without requiring precision gestures.
